08 · Complete Production Suite

21 CLI Tools, 10 Craft Guides & Native Engine Exports

Core Generators & Layering

  • pixel_studio.py — Unified master CLI + preset/palette inspector
  • character_gen.py — Multi-layer 1/2/4/8-dir characters (8px..64px+, 2..60f)
  • layer_compose.py — Layer toggle, opacity, blend modes & .aseprite export
  • tileset_gen.py — Seamless terrain, Wang 16, Blob 47, animated & isometric
  • vfx_gen.py — 12 seeded procedural pixel art VFX generators
  • asset_gen.py — 10 RPG items, ZzSprite creatures & 5 UI components
  • sprite_stack.py — Fake-3D slice strips & 360° turntables
  • sprite_clean.py — Deterministic messy/AI PNG cleanup pipeline

Post-Processing, QA & Engine Export

  • atlas_pack.py — Aseprite JSON, Phaser, Unity, Godot 4 .tres, .aseprite
  • quality_audit.py & batch_audit.py — AA, orphans, jaggies, seamless, palette QA
  • outline_generator.py — Outside, inset, selective (selout) & rim-light
  • gif_export.py — Transparent animated GIF (1..60 FPS, nearest scaling)
  • export_indexed.py — Alpha-preserving indexed 8-bit PNG
  • palette_remap.py & palette_extract.py — Perceptual remapping & extraction
  • dither.py & noise_generator.py — Bayer 2/4/8, band-edge, toroidal FBM/Voronoi
  • sprite_resize.py, sprite_mirror.py, init_workspace.py
